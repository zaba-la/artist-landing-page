About me

The story so far

I grew up in Valencia, between the sea and my grandmother's old record player. I picked up the guitar at twelve and haven't put it down since.

My songs mix Mediterranean folk with the quiet storytelling of North American songwriters. I write about small moments — a train window, a kitchen at 2 a.m., a letter never sent — and try to make them feel big.

Career

  1. 2018

    First open mic

    Started playing open-mic nights in Valencia's Ruzafa neighbourhood and recorded a first demo at home.

  2. 2021

    Debut album Salt & Cedar

    Self-released debut of ten songs. The single “Harbour Lights” reached one million streams.

  3. 2023

    Festival stages

    Opened for touring acts across Spain and played my first summer festivals.

  4. 2024

    Second album Lanterns in the Fog

    Recorded live to tape with a small string section. Named one of the year's best folk records by independent radio.
